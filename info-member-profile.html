Your member profile — optional information

When you sign up, you may choose to save the following in your members portal, so the assistant can fill it into forms for you — if you choose the fill option. Every item is optional.

Name, address, date, date of birth, location of incidents, county, state, ZIP code, and — for Pro members, when necessary — case number.

None of these are mandatory. You do not have to provide any of them to sign up, ask questions, or use the library.

These items can be filled out in your members portal at any time — when you sign up, later, or never. You can add, change, or remove them at any time.

For Pro pack assistant information: saving these items means the assistant does not have to keep asking you to fill in blanks. When you choose the fill option, the saved information goes into the proper blanks on the pre-filled printout — including the case number, when you have added one.
