How to file a complaint

How to file a complaint

Pick your state in the lookup above to get the disciplinary line and mailing address for where your complaint goes.

California

  • File online at apps.calbar.ca.gov/complaint — up to 10 attachments, 4 MB each.
  • Or mail to: Office of Chief Trial Counsel Intake, 845 South Figueroa Street, Los Angeles, CA 90017.
  • Questions: 800-843-9053.

Texas

  • File online at sbotservices.texasbar.com — the current grievance form is required.
  • Fax: (512) 427-4315.
  • Or mail to: Chief Disciplinary Counsel’s Office, P.O. Box 13287, Austin, TX 78711.
  • Helpline: 1-800-932-1900.

Good to know before you file

  • Complaints are free.
  • Complaints are confidential unless charges are filed.
  • Use the current form — old forms get sent back.
  • A grievance names an individual attorney, not a firm.
