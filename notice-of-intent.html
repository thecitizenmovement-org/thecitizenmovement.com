Notice of Intent

The United Citizens of America — DRAFT for founder review. Not yet final.

The United Citizens of America is a non-partisan movement grounded in the Constitution and the Bill of Rights. Our purpose is to stop political corruption, malfeasance, and misprision, and to advocate for citizens with civil-rights violations, police misconduct complaints, and family members killed by police.

We intend to:

  • Document. Receive citizen reports, preserve the originals, and keep a who-did-what record with dates, sources, and hashes. Nothing is deleted or altered; originals stay untouched.
  • Audit. Measure what government offices did — and did not do — against the law that governs them: public-records duties, charging and investigation duties, Brady and Giglio disclosure duties, and due process. Each audit item states what is verified, what is the citizen's account, and what is still to verify.
  • Publish. Post investigative alerts on the County & State Non-Compliance Board and case records on the Justice Wall. A mark is an investigative alert, not a finding and not a court conclusion.
  • Seek acknowledgment and remedy. Put the record before the officials and offices responsible — and before the Attorney General, the Department of Justice, state attorneys general, grand juries, and the courts — and ask for acknowledgment of awareness and for remedial action.

How we work. We are citizens, journalists, and auditors — not a court and not a prosecutor. We do not file suits, build criminal cases, or threaten anyone with criminal liability. Liability is a question for an attorney and for the courts. What we do is build the record, keep it intact, and make it public so that the offices responsible, and the public, can see it and act on it.

What this notice is not. It is not a finding against any person or office, not a court conclusion, and not a threat. It is a public statement of what this movement intends to do, openly and on the record, so that no official can later say they were not aware.

— The United Citizens of America
Investigative Journalist, Civil Rights Advocate
