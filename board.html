Pin color code

The movement is nonpartisan — no party colors, no party geography on this map. Dots are locators: red while non-compliant, gold while coming soon, light as a scar once compliant.

  • ● Dot on — Non-compliant (investigative alert, not a finding or court conclusion)
  • ● Gold dot — Coming soon (a county the team is compiling; not marked yet, no finding)
  • Light dot — Previously non-compliant (when a county comes into compliance, its red dot fades to the same light dot already on the map — a scar, not a disappearance; no red means no live alert)

No dot at all means no reviewed report posted yet — not a finding of compliance.
