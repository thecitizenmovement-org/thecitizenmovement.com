What is the movement?

Non-partisan, grounded in the Constitution and Bill of Rights. Mission: stop political corruption, malfeasance, and misprision; advocate for unheard citizens with civil-rights violations, police misconduct complaints, and families of people killed by police. Full mission statement and vision are on the .org site.

How do I become a member?

Membership is free. Create a free account on the store, and you are a member. Paid packs are separate — membership itself costs nothing. Sign-up link: the store.

What does membership do?

Members can submit a county or state for non-compliance review, use the members library, and ask the assistant once they have purchased a pack. Members do not decide marks — the founder reviews every submission before any mark appears. A mark is an investigative alert, not a finding.

Your member profile — optional information

Name, address, date, date of birth, location of incidents, county, state, ZIP code, and — for Pro members, when necessary — case number. None of these are mandatory. They can be filled out in your members portal at any time. For Pro pack assistant information: saving these items means the assistant does not have to keep asking you to fill in blanks. When you choose the fill option, the saved information goes into the proper blanks on the pre-filled printout — including the case number, when you have added one.

Legal assistance for pro se (self-represented) litigants — not legal advice.
