Read this first

A mark is an investigative alert — a place for the investigations team and auditors to look. It is not a finding and not a court conclusion. Citizens report. An unapproved report does not go up.

Each item below is shown in three tiers, kept distinct:

  • (A) Verified fact — confirmed against a primary source (an official document, a court docket, a named news report, or a document on file that was independently checked).
  • (B) Josh's account — Joshua Caleb Scott's findings, in his own words as recorded. His plain statements are his findings. They are presented as his account, never as independently verified fact.
  • (C) Still to verify — unconfirmed, a lead from a single source, or a document not yet read. Nothing in this tier is stated as fact.

An audit finding at the end of each item measures it against its law code. "Non-compliant in part" means it does not meet the standard on the current record; "compliant in form" means it does; "cannot determine" means the record is incomplete. No audit finding draws a fault conclusion beyond what it states. Liability is an attorney question.
