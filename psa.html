What Shasta County Is Saying — Anderson Parks Rent Pause

Posted November 11, 2026. This announcement reports what residents of Shasta County said in a public community discussion on November 10, 2026, about a rent increase at two Shasta County mobile home parks. Private residents are not named. The founder’s own words are quoted as his account.

The story

A planned $300 rent increase at two Shasta County mobile home parks is paused for 90 days. Residents are asking: what happens on day 91? They want written protection during the pause, transparency, and a real commitment to a permanent rent stabilization agreement. As one resident put it: this issue is bigger than one property owner or one rent increase — it affects mobile home owners throughout Shasta County.

What the people said

Thirteen comments in the discussion. The issues the people raised:

  • Redding needs the same protection. A Redding resident said landlords there raise rent every year, and Redding needs the pause too.
  • California’s rent-increase limits. A resident said the law allows rent to be raised twice a year by 5% each time, or once a year by 10% — and called it price gouging.
  • The pause should be permanent. A resident said it should be a permanent pause, not 90 days.
  • REU power rates and shutoffs. The founder: REU “just decides it’s going to fluctuate your power rate” and there is no other choice — a monopoly. A mother with two kids and one on the way had her power shut off because her payment plan lands two days after her bill every month.
  • CPS, social workers, and the judges. A resident asked whether the movement is looking into the Department of Health and Human Services — CPS and social workers — and the judges who handle CPS cases.
  • The answer given: CPS and social workers are being investigated. The child support division is being investigated. Judges have been touched on briefly; law enforcement and the District Attorney’s offices are covered. The investigation keeps expanding.
  • Cost of living, then vs now. The founder posted the 1983 vs 2026 poster: gas $1.24 vs $5.89, a new car $8,577 vs $48,000, a median home $82,600 vs $430,000, minimum wage $3.35 vs $16.50.

What happens now

Thirteen new Shasta County polls are live on the Polling Station — one for each issue the people raised. Members sign in to vote. One vote per poll per member. Anonymous — nobody sees who voted how.

  • Should Shasta County mobile home parks have permanent rent stabilization?
  • Should Redding stop landlords from raising rent every year?
  • Do you believe landlords in Shasta County are price gouging with rent increases?
  • Should Redding Electric Utility’s rates and shutoff practices be investigated?
  • Should Shasta County CPS and its social workers be investigated?
  • Should the judges who handle CPS cases be investigated?
  • Should the child support division be investigated?
  • Have you or someone you know had a vehicle stolen in Shasta County that was never recovered?
  • Should Shasta County Registrar Clint Curtis be removed from office?
  • Have you or someone you know been falsely accused or denied justice in Shasta County?
  • Have you had a case in Shasta County that was never solved?
  • Do you believe Shasta County has misplaced or misspent public money?
  • Is life in Shasta County harder now than it was in 1983?
