Attorneys Complaint Board

The United Citizens of America — a public record of attorney file-request history and citizen complaints

Nothing on this page is a finding of misconduct. A missed deadline or an unanswered file request is a record fact. A claim of misconduct is an allegation unless a court, the State Bar, or an official record confirms it. Liability is an attorney question. We do not file suits, build criminal cases, or threaten anyone with criminal liability.

How to read this board

Every entry carries the same three tiers, so the reader can tell what is proven, what is a citizen's account, and what is still open.

Verified — what the record showsDates, letters sent, replies received, deadlines passed, and official records, each with its source. A deadline that passed in silence is a record fact.
Citizen's account — what Josh or Robert saysTheir findings, in their words, presented as their account — never as independently verified fact.
Still to verifyWhat is missing, unknown, or unconfirmed. If it is not in the sources, it is listed here, not asserted.

The board covers the attorneys already named in Josh's and Robert Gibbs's files — his defense counsel, his prosecutors, and Robert's defense counsel, prosecutors, and other named attorneys. Attorneys named in the files who are not subjects of any complaint are listed at the bottom as not-board-subjects. Sitting judges named in the same allegations are noted separately; they are not attorney entries.
