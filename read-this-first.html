Members portal — optional profile

Your information, only if you want to save it.

When you sign up, you may choose to save the following information in your members portal, so the assistant can fill it into forms for you — if you choose the fill option. Every item below is optional.

Optional items you may save

— Full name
— Address
— Date
— Date of birth
— Location of incidents
— County
— State
— ZIP code
— Case number (Pro members only, when necessary)

For Pro pack assistant information: saving these items means the assistant does not have to keep asking you to fill in blanks. When you choose the fill option, the saved information goes into the proper blanks on the pre-filled printout — including the case number, when you have added one.

Disclaimer — please read

None of these items are mandatory. You do not have to provide any of them to sign up, to ask questions, or to use the members library.

These items can be filled out in your members portal at any time — when you sign up, later, or never. You can add, change, or remove them at any time.

If you do save them, they are used only to fill forms with the information you provided, in the proper blanks, when you choose the fill option. Nothing is invented or guessed. You review any filled form before it is filed. The case number field is for Pro members, added only when necessary, so it can appear on the pre-filled printout.
