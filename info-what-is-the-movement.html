What is the movement?

Non-partisan, grounded in the Constitution and Bill of Rights.

Mission: stop political corruption, malfeasance, and misprision; advocate for unheard citizens with civil-rights violations, police misconduct complaints, and families of people killed by police.

Vision: an informed citizenry that can read the record and hold officials to it, with receipts.

Founder principles: preservation-first — erase nothing, originals stay untouched, work happens on copies; accountability through receipts — hashed originals, custody logs, published records.
