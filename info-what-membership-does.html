What does membership do?

Members can submit a county or state for non-compliance review, use the members library, and ask the assistant once they have purchased a pack.

Members do not decide marks — the founder reviews every submission before any mark appears. A mark is an investigative alert, not a finding.

Membership does not give legal advice, and does not file anything on your behalf.
